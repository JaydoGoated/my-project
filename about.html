Why we built this

Roho Safi was built to support Kenyan Grade 9 (KPSEA) and Form 4 (KCSE) candidates during the most stressful weeks of their school year — mocks, exams, and results release. Most candidates face this pressure without confidential support or trained counselors nearby, and many avoid seeking help due to stigma. This tool offers private, self-guided coping strategies and access to real support, so no one has to face exam season alone.

What our research found

According to the 2022 Kenya National Adolescent Mental Health Survey, 44.3% of Kenyan adolescents report mental health challenges. A separate study of 2,907 Kenyan high school students found that over half had never considered seeking mental health support outside their family, with professional sources being the least-used option. Research also shows avoidance is the most common coping strategy among Kenyan secondary students, largely driven by fear of stigma — which is why Roho Safi is designed to be private and judgment-free.
