"Kenyan Grade 9 (KJSEA) and Form 4 (KCSE) candidates in under-resourced public schools cannot get confidential mental health support during mocks, exams, and results release — because schools lack trained counselors, and existing helplines go unused due to stigma and low awareness."

Why this matters

Over 68% of Kenyan candidates experience stress or panic before major exams like the KCSE.

The tool

This tool helps students identify their current feelings and provides coping strategies to manage stress, anxiety, and other emotions during critical academic periods.
