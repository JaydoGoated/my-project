Introduction

Welcome to our mental health tool designed specifically for Kenyan Grade 9 and Form 4 students. This tool aims to provide coping strategies to help you navigate the challenges of exam season and maintain your mental well-being.

What is mental health?

Mental health refers to our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how we think, feel, and act, and it plays a crucial role in our ability to handle stress, relate to others, and make choices.

Why this matters

Mental health matters because 44.3% to 68% of Kenyan adolescents experience mental health challenges — and for Grade 9 and Form 4 candidates, exam season adds a pressure most face without confidential support, trained counselors, or a safe way to ask for help.

The tool

This tool helps students identify their current feelings and provides coping strategies to manage stress, anxiety, and other emotions during critical academic periods.
