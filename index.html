Project title: ROHO SAFI

Kenyan Grade 9 (KPSEA) and Form 4 (KCSE) candidates in under-resourced public schools cannot get confidential mental health support during mocks, exams, and results release — because schools lack trained counselors, and existing helplines go unused due to stigma and low awareness.

Why this matters

Over 68% of Kenyan candidates experience stress or panic before major exams like the KCSE.
